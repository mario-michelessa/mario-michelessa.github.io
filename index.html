About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the National University of Singapore, advised by Brian Y. Lim and Christophe Hurter. Before my PhD, I completed an MSc by Research in Statistics and Applied Probability at NUS and an engineering degree at CentraleSupélec, Université Paris-Saclay. I am fortunate to be supported by the AI Singapore–Descartes Joint PhD Scholarship, part of a French–Singaporean research programme led by CNRS@CREATE.

Research

My main research is human-AI collaboration. I study how people can effectively control and verify AI systems operating over large and heterogeneous collections of data. I develop interactive methods that make AI behavior task-specific, inspectable, and revisable.

On the side, I am interested in theoretical AI, including transformer expressivity and prompt tuning. I am also interested in applications to aviation and healthcare.
